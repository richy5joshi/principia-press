About This E-Book

This handbook is designed for B.Tech ECE, EEE, EIE and related engineering students, freshers and competitive-exam aspirants who want to strengthen their Digital Electronics fundamentals and prepare for technical interviews and academic assessments.


The series follows a practical question-and-answer approach rather than a conventional theory-only textbook. It focuses on important Digital Electronics concepts, interview questions, explanations and quick revision.

What You Will Learn

Number Systems

Binary, Decimal, Octal and Hexadecimal number systems, conversions, complements and signed number representations.

Logic Gates

Basic logic gates, universal gates, XOR/XNOR and their applications.

Boolean Algebra

Boolean laws, De Morgan's theorems, SOP, POS and logic simplification.

K-Maps

Karnaugh Maps, grouping rules, don't-care conditions and logic minimization.

Combinational Circuits

MUX, DEMUX, encoders, decoders, adders, subtractors, comparators and ALU basics.

Sequential Circuits

Latches, flip-flops, registers, counters and Moore/Mealy FSMs.

Timing Concepts

Setup time, hold time, clock skew, critical path, slack and timing violations.

Interview Preparation

Frequently asked questions, practical explanations, interview tips and quick revision concepts.

The 5-Day Structure

Day 1 of 5

Number Systems & Logic Gates

Binary, Decimal, Octal and Hexadecimal systems, number conversions, 1's and 2's complements, signed representations, logic gates, NAND, NOR, XOR, XNOR and binary arithmetic.

Day 2 of 5

Boolean Algebra & K-Maps

Boolean laws, De Morgan's theorems, SOP and POS, canonical forms, minterms, maxterms, K-Map grouping, prime implicants and don't-care conditions.

Day 3 of 5

Combinational Circuits

Multiplexers, demultiplexers, encoders, priority encoders, decoders, half and full adders, subtractors, comparators, BCD conversion and ALU basics.

Day 4 of 5

Sequential Circuits & FSMs

Latches, flip-flops, flip-flop conversions, triggering, race-around condition, registers, counters and Moore/Mealy finite-state machines.

Day 5 of 5

Timing Concepts

Setup time, hold time, clock skew, critical path, maximum frequency, slack, WNS, TNS, timing violations and common fixes.
