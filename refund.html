Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains the refund and cancellation conditions applicable to digital e-books and other digital products offered by Principia Press.

1. Introduction

Principia Press provides technical e-books and digital educational publications through its website.

Because our products are primarily digital products that may be delivered electronically after successful payment, refunds and cancellations are subject to the conditions described below.

2. Digital Products

Our e-books and other digital publications are supplied in electronic formats. Once a payment has been successfully completed, access or delivery of the purchased digital product may be initiated electronically.

Important

Please review the product title, description and available information before completing your purchase.

3. Cancellation Policy

A purchase may generally be cancelled only before the digital product has been delivered or access has been provided, subject to the applicable payment provider's processing status.

Once a digital product has been delivered or access has been provided, cancellation may no longer be possible because the digital content may already have been accessed or downloaded.

4. Refund Policy

Refund requests for digital products are considered on a case-by-case basis.

Refunds May Be Considered For

Duplicate payments, technical delivery failures, or other genuine transaction-related issues may be reviewed for a possible refund.

Digital Product Access

Refund requests based solely on a change of mind after successful delivery or access to a digital product may not be accepted.

Any refund decision will depend on the circumstances of the transaction and the information available to Principia Press.

5. Failed or Pending Payments

If money has been debited from your account but the order has not been successfully completed or the product has not been delivered, please contact us with the relevant transaction details.

We will review the transaction status and, where appropriate, coordinate with the payment service provider to resolve the issue.

6. Payment Service Providers

Payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers such as Razorpay or PhonePe, depending on the payment method made available on the website.

Payment processing, transaction status and certain refund procedures may also be subject to the applicable policies and terms of the payment service provider.

7. Digital Delivery Issues

If you have completed a payment but have not received the purchased digital product, please contact us.

We will review the payment and delivery status and attempt to resolve legitimate delivery problems.

8. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or report a payment or delivery issue, contact Principia Press using the official email address below.

Official Email

principiapressofficial@gmail.com

Please include your name, purchased product, date of transaction and relevant transaction/order information.

9. Changes to This Policy

Principia Press may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy from time to time to reflect changes in products, payment methods, delivery systems or applicable requirements.

The updated version will be published on this page.

10. Effective Date

This Refund & Cancellation Policy is effective from September 2026.
