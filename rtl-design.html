About This E-Book

RTL Design 100 FAQs for Interview Prep is a question-focused technical guide designed for candidates preparing for RTL Design and Frontend VLSI interviews.


The book focuses on practical RTL design concepts, Verilog programming, FSM design, timing concepts, synthesis and coding practices. It is structured around 100 interview questions to support focused preparation and quick revision.
