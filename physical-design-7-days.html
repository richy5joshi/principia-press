About This E-Book

Physical Design is the backend stage of VLSI design where the logical design is transformed into a physical layout suitable for manufacturing. This 7-Day Physical Design series takes the reader through the major stages of the Physical Design flow, beginning with the fundamentals and progressing toward verification, signoff, advanced topics and career guidance.


The series is structured to provide a clear understanding of the Physical Design journey while connecting fundamental concepts with industry-oriented topics.

What You Will Learn

Physical Design Fundamentals

Understand Physical Design, backend VLSI, die/core concepts and PPA fundamentals.

Floorplanning

Learn floorplanning, macro placement and power planning concepts.

Placement

Understand global, legal and detailed placement along with congestion analysis.

Clock Tree Synthesis

Learn clock distribution, skew, latency, buffers and CTS objectives.

Routing

Understand global routing, detailed routing, tracks, vias and metal layers.

Physical Verification

Signoff

Physical Design Flow

The series connects the major stages involved in transforming the synthesized logical design into a physical implementation.

Synthesis → Floorplanning → Placement → CTS → Routing → Physical Verification → Signoff → GDSII / Tapeout

Industry & Interview Focus

The handbook also introduces practical concepts that are important for Physical Design interviews and real-world backend VLSI work. Topics include DRC versus LVS, antenna violations, IR drop, electromigration, ECOs, routing congestion, timing signoff, metal density and tapeout checks.


The book also discusses industry tools and skills associated with Physical Design, including Cadence Innovus, Synopsys ICC2, Calibre, Python/TCL scripting and advanced-node concepts.
