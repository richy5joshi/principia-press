Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Principia Press handles information when you visit or use this website and when you purchase or interact with our digital publications.

1. Introduction

Principia Press is an independent technical publishing initiative focused on technical educational resources and digital publications in areas including Electronics, VLSI, FPGA, Verilog and digital design.

We respect your privacy and aim to handle personal information responsibly.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with Principia Press, information that may be provided to us can include:

  • Your name and email address when you contact us.
  • Information necessary to process or support an e-book purchase.
  • Information that you voluntarily provide when communicating with Principia Press.

We do not intentionally request unnecessary personal information through this website.

3. Payment Information

When payment functionality is enabled, payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers such as Razorpay or PhonePe.

Principia Press does not intend to directly collect or store your complete debit card, credit card, banking or UPI credentials.

Payment information is handled according to the applicable privacy policies and security practices of the payment provider used for the transaction.

4. How We Use Information

Information provided to Principia Press may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to enquiries and support requests.
  • Processing and supporting e-book purchases.
  • Providing purchased digital products.
  • Communicating with customers regarding their transactions or support requests.
  • Improving the website and our publications.

5. Third-Party Services

Principia Press may use third-party services for website hosting, payment processing, communication, analytics or other necessary website functions.

These third-party services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Where appropriate, links to relevant third-party privacy policies may be provided when those services are integrated into the website.

6. Cookies and Tracking

The current Principia Press website does not intentionally use cookies for user account authentication or advertising purposes.

If analytics, payment services or other third-party technologies are added in the future, those services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information provided to Principia Press.

However, no method of transmission or storage over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, provide services, maintain transaction records, comply with applicable legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

9. Children's Privacy

Principia Press does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through the website.

10. External Links

The website may contain links to third-party websites or services.

Principia Press is not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of external websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party website you visit.

11. Updates to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the website, services, payment systems, legal requirements or business practices.

The updated version will be published on this page with the revised effective date.
