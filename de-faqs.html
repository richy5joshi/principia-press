About the E-Book

Digital Electronics forms one of the fundamental foundations for electronics, RTL design, FPGA and VLSI engineering.

This e-book is structured as a large question bank for interview preparation, allowing readers to revise important Digital Electronics concepts through question-oriented preparation.

Instead of searching through multiple notes, websites and scattered resources, the questions are organized in one place for faster preparation and revision.

What You Will Get

The question bank is intended to strengthen the fundamental Digital Electronics concepts commonly required before moving deeper into RTL and VLSI design.

Number Systems

Binary, decimal, hexadecimal, conversions and signed-number concepts.

Logic Gates

Boolean Algebra
