About This E-Book

This e-book is designed as a question-focused preparation resource for students, freshers and aspiring VLSI engineers who want to strengthen their Digital Electronics fundamentals and prepare for technical interviews.


Instead of searching through multiple notes, websites and scattered resources, the questions are organized into one dedicated preparation resource for faster revision and self-assessment.


The question-bank approach allows you to test your understanding of important Digital Electronics concepts and identify areas that require further revision.
