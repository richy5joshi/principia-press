About the E-Book

Digital Electronics is one of the fundamental areas commonly encountered in electronics and VLSI technical interviews. This e-book brings together 200+ frequently asked questions in a compact interview-preparation format.

Instead of searching through multiple notes and resources, the questions are organized in one place to support faster preparation, revision and self-testing.

What You Will Cover

The guide focuses on the core Digital Electronics concepts that form the foundation for further studies in RTL, FPGA and VLSI design.

Number Systems

Binary, decimal, hexadecimal and other fundamental number-system concepts used in digital design.

Boolean Algebra

Boolean expressions, laws, simplification and important logic-design principles.

Combinational Logic

MUX, DEMUX, encoders, decoders, adders, subtractors and related circuits.
